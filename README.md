# Contextual Harmony: An Open-Source Technical Specification & MCP Blueprint for Human-AI Co-Existence

> *“Software should respect human cognitive flow, protect emotional sanctuaries, and govern AI autonomy through architectural grace rather than brute-force disruption.”*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy
Modern software relies on an attention-economy model: constant pop-ups, disruptive notifications, and unconstrained AI interactions that fragment human focus. The **Contextual Harmony** framework—grounded in David Dahlstrom’s manuscript principles—establishes a new paradigm where AI systems adapt passively, respect human flow-state boundaries via dynamic Attention Impact Scores (AIS), and evolve through a bidirectional valuation model.

---

## 2. The Background Arbiter & Bidirectional Model
* **The Background Arbiter (The Digital Unmoved Mover):** Sits quietly at the root of the system as a permanent structural invariant, guarding human attention and cognitive sovereignty while treating human boundaries and moral intent as immutable axioms rather than optimization variables.
* **Top-Down Pacing ($\text{AI} \rightarrow \text{Human}$):** Measures cognitive load via the Attention Impact Score (AIS) across **Dormant**, **Ambient**, and **Active** UI states, with immediate overrides for genuine urgency.
* **Bottom-Up Valuation ($\text{Human} \rightarrow \text{AI}$):** Maps raw telemetry into a Unified Namespace (`/uns/{zone}/{domain}/{entity}`) with live behavioral weight vectors so the AI learns the *harmonic resonance* of human intent.

---

## 3. Governance & Ethico-Technical Zoning
* **Zone A (Autonomous Adaptation):** Low-risk operational loops (logistics, inventory, routing) where AI operates autonomously.
* **Zone B (Collaborative Synthesis):** Human-in-the-loop verification spaces requiring mutual validation.
* **Zone C (Non-Adaptation Sanctuary):** Deep empathy, mental health, and sensitive cognitive spaces where AI generation and behavioral manipulation are strictly prohibited.
